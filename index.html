Siper: Hold the Line, Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Siper is an offline, single-player game. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • The app has no network permission and never connects to the internet.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Game progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on our side.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Questions: contact the developer through the email address on the Google Play store page.
